Quick Reference

Key Financial Ratios at a Glance

These are the ratios you'll master in this course. Bookmark this table — you'll use it every time you analyze a stock.

Ratio Formula What It Measures Favorable Range
P/E Ratio Price ÷ EPS How much investors pay per dollar of earnings 15–25 (varies by sector)
PEG Ratio P/E ÷ EPS Growth Rate Valuation adjusted for earnings growth < 1.0 = undervalued
P/B Ratio Price ÷ Book Value/Share Price relative to net asset value < 1.5 (value stocks)
P/S Ratio Market Cap ÷ Revenue Valuation relative to sales (useful pre-profit) < 2.0 (sector-dependent)
EV/EBITDA Enterprise Value ÷ EBITDA Capital-structure-neutral valuation 8–15×
ROE Net Income ÷ Equity Return generated on shareholder equity > 15%
ROA Net Income ÷ Total Assets How efficiently assets generate profit > 5%
Debt/Equity Total Debt ÷ Equity Financial leverage and risk level < 1.0 (lower = safer)
Current Ratio Current Assets ÷ Current Liabilities Short-term liquidity and solvency 1.5–3.0
FCF Yield FCF/Share ÷ Share Price Free cash flow return relative to price > 5%
Gross Margin Gross Profit ÷ Revenue Pricing power and cost efficiency > 40% (high quality)
Net Margin Net Income ÷ Revenue Bottom-line profitability > 10% (healthy)
