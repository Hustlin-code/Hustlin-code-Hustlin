About Hustlin'

Hustlin' (hustlin.org) is a financial education platform for people building from the ground up. We publish structured, self-paced courses on financial literacy, technical analysis, and investing - written in plain language, with no jargon and no judgment.

Stage 1 of the Financial Literacy course opens with no account at all — just click and read. A free Hustlin' account tracks your progress and unlocks the rest of the course content. You can sign up with an email address and password, or sign in with Google. When you choose Google, we request only your name, email address, and profile picture - enough to create your account and show you which courses you have access to. We never post on your behalf and never access any other Google data.

Financial Literacy Stage 1 is open to everyone, no account needed, and the remaining Financial Literacy stages are free once you sign up. Stage 1 of every paid course is free with an account; full courses are a one-time payment. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
