Hustlin' · hustlin.org · Last updated: 12 August 2026

1. Introduction

Hustlin' ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website hustlin.org (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our Site. Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of the Site.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in the following ways:

  • Email address — if you voluntarily subscribe to updates through our email signup form.
  • Usage data — automatically collected information including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, and time spent on pages.
  • Cookies and tracking technologies — small files placed on your device. See Section 4 for full details.
  • Local storage — if you use the Site without an account, your course progress (completed modules and stages) is saved locally in your browser's localStorage on your own device, and is not sent to us.
  • Account information — if you create an account, we store your email address and a securely hashed version of your password. We never see or store your password in readable form.
  • Course progress on our servers — for account holders, completed modules and stages are also saved to our database so your progress follows you between devices.
  • Purchase records — if you buy a course, we store a record of what you bought and when, so we know what to unlock for you. We never receive or store your card number. Card details go to our payment processor, not to us.

Calculators are different. Every calculator on this Site runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you type into them are never sent anywhere. We do not see them, log them or save them. Close the tab and they are gone.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Send email updates and course notifications (only if you opt in).
  • Understand how visitors use our Site so we can improve it.
  • Serve relevant advertisements through Google AdSense (see Section 4).
  • Create and secure your account, and unlock the courses you have bought.
  • Save and restore your course progress across your devices.
  • Keep records of purchases for accounting and tax purposes.
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

4. Cookies and Google AdSense

This Site uses cookies — small text files stored in your browser — to enhance your experience and to serve advertisements.

Google AdSense: We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on this Site. Google AdSense uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You may also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

For more information on how Google uses data from sites that use its services, visit: How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

Other cookies we use:

  • Essential cookies — required for the Site to function, including saving your course progress in localStorage.
  • Analytics cookies — we may use tools to understand how visitors interact with the Site.

5. Cookie Consent for Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent message appears before advertising and measurement cookies are set. It is served through a Consent Management Platform certified by Google under the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework.

You can accept, decline, or open Manage options and choose purpose by purpose and vendor by vendor. Declining does not remove access to anything on this Site — every course, calculator, guide and market page stays open. It means the ads you see are non-personalised rather than personalised.

Your choice is stored in your browser so it is respected on later visits. You can change or withdraw it at any time using the privacy or cookie settings link the consent tool places on the page. Cookies that are strictly necessary for the Site to work, including the localStorage entry that saves your course progress on your own device, are not covered by that choice because the Site cannot function without them.

8. Your Rights and Choices

  • Email unsubscribe — every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. Click it at any time to remove yourself from our list.
  • Opt out of personalized ads — visit Google Ads Settings or NAI opt-out.
  • Delete local data — your course progress is stored in your browser's localStorage. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings (Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data).

9. Your Rights Under the GDPR and UK GDPR

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, data protection law gives you specific rights over personal data we hold about you. Those rights are to access a copy of it, to have inaccurate data rectified, to have it erased, to restrict or object to how it is processed, and to receive it in a portable, machine-readable format. Where processing relies on your consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time without affecting processing already carried out.

We rely on three legal bases. Consent covers advertising and measurement cookies for visitors in those regions, and email updates, which are opt-in and carry an unsubscribe link in every message. Legitimate interests cover keeping the Site secure and understanding in aggregate how it is used. Contract covers delivering a course you have paid for.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@hustlin.org. We will respond within one month. We will not charge you for making a request or treat you differently for having made one. If you believe we have handled your data improperly, you may complain to your national supervisory authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office.

10. Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA gives you the right to know what personal information we collect and why, to request that we delete it, to have inaccurate information corrected, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and to not be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights.

On "sale" and "sharing." We do not sell personal information for money. However, serving personalised advertising through Google AdSense involves disclosing identifiers such as cookie IDs and IP address to advertising partners for cross-context behavioral advertising, and California law treats that as "sharing." You can opt out in two ways: use the privacy or cookie settings link the consent tool places on the page to turn off personalised advertising, and enable a Global Privacy Control signal in your browser or extension, which we honor as a valid opt-out request.

The categories we handle are identifiers (IP address, cookie identifiers, and your email address if you subscribe), internet activity (pages viewed and referring URLs), and, for purchases, transaction records held by our payment processor. We do not knowingly collect the sensitive personal information categories the CPRA defines, and we do not collect information from anyone we know to be under 13.

To make a request, email privacy@hustlin.org with "California privacy request" in the subject line. We will verify the request against information we already hold — usually the email address involved — and respond within 45 days, extending once if the request is complex. An authorized agent may act for you with written permission.

11. Data Security and How Long We Keep Things

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. Passwords are stored hashed, never in readable form, and card details never reach us at all. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

Retention. We keep what we need for as long as we need it, and no longer:

  • Email list — until you unsubscribe, then deleted within 30 days.
  • Account and course progress — for as long as you have an account. Ask us to delete it and it goes within 30 days.
  • Purchase records — seven years after the purchase, because tax law requires it. This is the one category we cannot delete on request.
  • Server logs — 90 days, then discarded.
  • Calculator inputs — never collected, so nothing to keep.
