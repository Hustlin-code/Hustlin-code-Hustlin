Quick Reference

Key Economic Indicators Cheat Sheet

The most market-moving data releases you'll track every week and month as an informed trader.

Indicator Frequency What It Measures Market Impact
Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP) Monthly Jobs added/lost in the economy, excluding farm workers 🔴 Very High
CPI (Consumer Price Index) Monthly Consumer-level inflation across a basket of goods and services 🔴 Very High
FOMC Rate Decision 8x/Year Federal funds target rate and forward policy guidance 🔴 Very High
GDP (Gross Domestic Product) Quarterly Total economic output — the broadest measure of economic health 🟠 High
PPI (Producer Price Index) Monthly Wholesale-level price changes; a leading indicator for CPI 🟠 High
Initial Jobless Claims Weekly New unemployment insurance filings — real-time labor market pulse 🟡 Medium
Retail Sales Monthly Consumer spending levels across retail and food categories 🟠 High
ISM Manufacturing PMI Monthly Factory activity levels; above 50 = expansion, below 50 = contraction 🟠 High
Consumer Confidence Monthly Consumer sentiment and expectations about economic conditions 🟡 Medium
Housing Starts Monthly New residential construction; reflects builder confidence and demand 🟡 Medium
